## Distributions that matter for LEAP-One's story

- **Droning is bimodal.** Ten of 25 teams scored exactly 15, two scored 0, and the median is
  40. Only ten teams scored above 100. HSM's 265 (6th) is a genuine strength, not a
  mid-table number dressed up.
- **Mass is nearly a given for the field.** Nine teams at 200, median 178, mean 157. Only
  three teams scored under 100 (ProjectRED 62, [R]overTech 35, HSM −22). HSM is the sole
  negative score — the sheet has no other team over the allowance.
- **Documentation is compressed.** 25 teams between 310.5 and 371.75; HSM's 364.25 is 7.5
  off the top. The qualification half (239.75) is joint-best; the final report (124.5,
  6th) is where the 7.5 went.
- **Probing splits the field.** Seven teams at the full 200, four at 0, HSM at 12.
- **Traverse** median is 114.5; HSM's 43 is 21st with two teams at 0 below.
- Qualification correlates only weakly with the final score (r ≈ 0.39); documentation
